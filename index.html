About

I'm an Engineering Manager & Tech Lead focused on spatial perception and machine learning — taking computer vision models from data systems and prototypes into reliable, real-time on-device products.

Currently at Google, I serve as a TLM on Perception for Android XR (World Sensing). Earlier across two stints at HTC VIVE, I led ML scene understanding, 3D room capture, tracking algorithms, and 3D data/labeling infrastructure across VIVE Focus Vision, XR Elite, Focus 3, and Cosmos.

Outside XR, I built large-scale recommendation systems as a Senior Data Scientist at Garena (BOOYAH! Live, lifting clip view duration by 10%) and Senior ML Engineer at Dcard. I hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from NTHU (advised by Prof. Chia-Wen Lin) and a B.S. in EE from CCU.
